# Third-party asset licenses

Policy (AGENTS.md, issue #246): third-party game art and audio are allowed only under **CC0** or **CC-BY**. Every file must live under `src/frontend/public/assets/third-party/<pack>/` next to its license text, be listed here and appear on the in-game Credits page (`/credits`, data in `src/frontend/src/features/credits/creditsData.ts`). CC-BY entries need visible attribution.
